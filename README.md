# 📊 Adventure Works Analytics | Tableau Dashboard

This project presents a complete **Sales & Profit Analysis** for the fictional Adventure Works company using **Tableau**.  
The dashboard provides insights into **profitability, sales performance, product trends, region-wise breakdowns, and customer demographics**.  
It helps business stakeholders make data-driven decisions using clear KPIs and interactive visuals.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to analyze Adventure Works' global sales data and identify key patterns related to:

- Total Sales & Total Profit  
- Region-wise performance  
- Best-selling products  
- Month & Quarter-wise revenue trends  
- Production cost vs. sales  
- Employee productivity  
- Gender-based sales distribution  
- Year-on-year growth

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### **📍 Country Wise Sales**
- United States and Australia are the top-performing regions.  
- Other major markets include UK, Germany, France, and Canada.  
Provides clarity on region-wise growth opportunities.

### **📅 Month Wise Sales vs Production Cost**
- Sales show an increasing trend from January to December.  
- December has the highest revenue (~₹3,212K).  
- Comparison with production cost identifies profitable months.

### **🏆 Top 10 Product Sales**
- Mountain Bikes and Road Bikes dominate the sales category.  
- Helps understand which product lines drive the most revenue.

### **📈 Year Wise Sales**
- Significant spike in 2013 (~₹16M).  
- A downward trend appears after 2014, requiring strategic evaluation.

### **🥇 Top 5 Product Categories**
- Road Bikes lead with highest revenue (~₹14,521K).  
- Mountain Bikes follow next, with other categories contributing smaller shares.

### **🟢 Quarter Wise Sales**
- Q1 and Q4 outperform other quarters.  
- Shows seasonal patterns in customer purchasing behavior.

### **👥 Gender Wise Sales**
- Male: ₹9.35K  
- Female: ₹9.13K  
Insights help with targeted marketing strategies.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

- **Tableau** – Data visualization & dashboard creation  
- **Excel/CSV** – Data source  
- **Data Cleaning** – Handling nulls, formatting, calculated fields  
- **ETL Steps** – Cleaning, Structuring, Filtering, Adding KPIs  

---

## 🔄 ETL & Data Preparation Process

1. **Extract**
   - Imported Adventure Works sales data (Excel/CSV format).  

2. **Transform**
   - Cleaned missing values  
   - Standardized date formats  
   - Created calculated fields (Profit, Tax, Total Sales, etc.)  
   - Created KPI indicators  
   - Removed duplicates  
   - Merged tables (Sales, Products, Customers where required)

3. **Load**
   - Loaded final dataset into Tableau  
   - Built visuals and created parameters & filters (Country, Year, Gender, etc.)
